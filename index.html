FOUR DECADES OF QUALITY

Engineering Security & Architectural Elegance

Founded in 1981 in Tala Nagari, Aligarh — the historical heart of India's lock manufacturing industry — Vinayak Locks has earned the trust of leading architects, builders, hardware merchants, and interior specialists.

Our manufacturing ethos combines advanced CNC tooling, precision zinc casting, computer-coded security brass pin cylinders, and multi-stage electroplated finishes that resist harsh climatic conditions and wear.

100% In-House Tested

Every batch undergoes strict cycle endurance, deadbolt torque, and salt-spray corrosion tests.

Bespoke Finishes

Available in Satin Chrome, Antique Brass, Matte Black, Copper, and Matt Antique Gold.
